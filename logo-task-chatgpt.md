# Task: app icon for "Zero"

You are designing the app icon for a mobile web app. Please generate images, not descriptions.

## About the app

**Zero** helps friends split shared expenses and track who owes whom: who paid, how the bill was divided, and what each person still owes. It runs in the browser and is installed on the iPhone and Android home screen like a regular app.

The name means the goal: bring every balance back to zero, so nobody owes anybody.

## What I need

A **symbol only** (no wordmark). The same symbol will be used as the app icon and as a small logo in the app header. I will typeset the word "Zero" myself, so do not draw any text.

## Design requirements

- One simple geometric symbol. No text, no letters, no digits other than a stylized zero if the concept is built on it.
- Flat style: solid fills only. No gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no gloss, no textures.
- At most two colors for the symbol, plus one plain background color.
- Must stay clearly readable at 32×32 pixels, so no thin lines and no small details.
- Should work on both light and dark backgrounds.
- Should not resemble the logo of any well-known company or app.

## Concept directions

Explore these, one variation per direction:

1. A bold zero / ring as the whole symbol.
2. A ring made of two halves that complete each other ("I pay, you pay").
3. Two arrows travelling around a circle and closing into a zero.
4. A balance scale reduced to a ring and a single line, perfectly level.

## Color

The app already uses green `#059669` for "you are owed" and red `#dc2626` for "you owe". Those two are taken for amounts, so please pick a **different** main color for the icon. Suggest what you think fits a friendly, trustworthy money app.

## Canvas rules (important)

- Square canvas, 1024×1024.
- Solid background color that fills the canvas edge to edge.
- **Do not round the corners** and do not draw an icon frame, border or device mockup. iOS and Android crop the icon themselves.
- Keep the symbol centered and inside the central 60% of the canvas. Android may crop the icon to a circle, so anything near the edge gets cut off.

## Step 1: variations

Generate 4 distinct variations, one per concept direction above, each as a separate 1024×1024 image following the canvas rules. Number them 1 to 4.

Then stop and wait for me to choose.

## Step 2: final files

After I pick a variation, keep its shape exactly the same and produce three separate files:

1. `icon-1024.png` — the symbol on the solid colored square background, 1024×1024, no transparency, square corners.
2. `mark.png` — the symbol alone on a transparent background, 1024×1024.
3. `mark-mono.png` — the same symbol in solid black on a transparent background, 1024×1024.

If you can also provide the symbol as clean SVG code (`mark.svg`), please do. Only do this if the SVG matches the image exactly; otherwise skip it.

Finally, tell me the exact hex codes you used:

- symbol color(s): `#RRGGBB`
- background color: `#RRGGBB`
